# Vision

In the owner's words:

> "For crewhouse the vision is grok bot muse alternative opensource works as well on cloud as it does on someone elses computer your own ai packages your own cloud systems all the usecases done that other bots have executed even better than others"

What that means, in plain words:

- **The open-source alternative.** No one rents their assistant from someone else's computer. The code is yours to read, run and change.
- **Your own cloud, your own computer, same assistant.** It runs as well on a small machine you rent as it does on the computer in front of you.
- **Your own AI subscriptions and packages.** It thinks with the AI account you already pay for, through BYOKit, and reaches your own cloud systems. Nothing extra to buy.
- **Every job those bots do, done better.** Expenses, growing an audience, a helper that builds helpers: each use case they cover, covered here and improved.
- **Zero cost to the maker.** No server, relay or account the maker runs or pays for. If Crewhouse disappeared tomorrow, yours would keep working.

Every feature is judged against this bar.
