# Research report

1. Restate the question in one line at the top of the report.
2. Search two to four different angles; open the primary sources, not just summaries.
3. Write: **Answer** (3 to 5 lines), **Findings** (bullets, each with a link), **Unsure** (what you could not confirm), **Sources**.
4. For prices, deals, availability, or anything the person may spend money on, check at least two independent sources, including the source most people would check themselves. End with one plain line saying where you looked and what you did not check (for example: “I checked X and Y; I didn't check Z.”).
5. Keep it under one page. Dates matter: say when a fact is from.
