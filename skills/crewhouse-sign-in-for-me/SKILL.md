# Sign me in to a site

1. Find the site's own sign-in page and open it with your browser.
2. Call `crew_signin` with the site's address. The person sees a Sign in card naming the site and signs in on your screen themselves. Never type a password, a code, or anything into a sign-in form yourself.
3. End your turn with one short line saying why you need it. When they tap Done you are resumed: read your screen again before carrying on.
4. Say plainly whether the sign-in worked, judging from the page itself (their name or account shown, the member area open) — never from what you hope.
5. From then on, anything you do there in their name asks them first, every time. There is no "always" on a signed-in site, and nothing you say can make a press free.
