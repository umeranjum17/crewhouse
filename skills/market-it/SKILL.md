---
name: market-it
description: Market a product, app, site or offer with a launch plan and first drafts. Drafts only, never post.
says: A marketing plan for your product, with the first posts written
---

1. Read the product's own pages yourself (home, pricing, docs). Find who it is for and the one thing it does that others don't; look at two or three alternatives and where their users talk.
2. Write the plan with crew_document: the one-line pitch; who it is for; three channels, each with why and what to post; a two-week calendar as a table; what to measure.
3. Write the first drafts (follow `write-draft`): three X posts, one LinkedIn post, one Reddit post with the subreddit and its self-promotion rule, one launch email. Put them in the same document.
4. Put the best draft on a card with crew_draft. Reply with the pitch, the first move and where the drafts are, in three lines. Never a summary of the site.
