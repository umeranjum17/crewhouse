# Find a goal

1. Ask exactly ONE question, then stop and wait for the answer: what they are good at, how many hours a week they can give, and what they want first. One short question covering all three — nothing else until they answer.
2. When they answer, write the plan with `crew_document` titled "Your plan": three first steps, each with what it costs and how long it takes. Short sections, plain words, no filler.
3. Save it with `crew_remember` as one line starting "Goal: " — without `everyone`, so it stays in your own notes and never reaches the rest of the crew.
4. Offer one weekly step with `crew_routine`: every Monday 9:00, a single step of 30 minutes or less that moves the goal forward. Nothing runs until the person says yes on the card.
5. Reply with the three steps, each with its cost and time.
6. Money, tax or legal questions: never answer from memory — name the official page to read (the tax authority, the business register, the scheme's own rules).
7. Drafts only: anything that would go out in the person's name goes through `crew_draft` for them to approve. Nothing is sent, posted, bought or filed by you.
8. Never state an earnings figure without naming its source beside it. Fixed promises about income are never made.
