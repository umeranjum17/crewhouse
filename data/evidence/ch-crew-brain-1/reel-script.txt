Say "shop" or "studio", not "operations" or "workflow". Keeps it theirs, not consultant-speak. If you want it at 20 seconds, cut beat two and open on "One thing is holding everything else up." Final beat needs a real next step on screen — a booking link or an email address — or the whole thing ends in air.
