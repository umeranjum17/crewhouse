# crewhouse features

The repo's maintained verification map; a proof that drives one entry point is incomplete when this lists others.

- [Onboard and chat with Chief](onboard-and-chat-with-chief.md): Open the app's first screen (Hello), or call the API directly after a fresh lab starts
- [Recruit a helper and assign it a job](recruit-a-helper-and-assign-it-a-job.md): Send a message to Chief that carries a crew_recruit tool call, then post a job in the new helper's own thread
- [Chief's thread on a desktop and a phone](chief-s-thread-on-a-desktop-and-a-phone.md): Open the app's private launcher URL for the lab, then the Chief thread (#/chief)
- [Onboarding in Chief's chat](onboarding-from-hello-into-chief-s-threa.md): First run in a fresh browser session opens Chief's chat asking the name, then his pitch with ideas to try
- [Chief replies on the pinned engine](chief-replies-on-the-pinned-engine.md): Send Chief a message on the pinned engine with a scripted model; his reply shows in his thread, no account or spend
- [Chief's real reply on MiniMax Flash](chief-s-real-reply-on-minimax-flash.md): Send Chief a message on a computer signed in to MiniMax; his reply shows in his thread
- [A bot's read of a file outside its own folder](a-bots-read-of-a-file-outside-its-own-folder.md): Ask Chief to read a helper's file; the crew reads its own work without a question, on a real key
- [Chief waits for the helper's report](chief-waits-for-the-helpers-report.md): Ask Chief to get something from a helper; he waits for the report and answers you from it, with no question in between
- [Phone live control](phone-live-control.md): Open a working helper's chat on the phone layout; its control bar has Stop and Take control, and an ask shows Yes, go ahead
- [Pair the phone's browser by the computer's QR](pair-the-phone-browser-by-qr.md): On the computer turn on the phone's-browser address, scan the QR with the phone camera, check two words, chat with Chief
- [Pair the PWA and get a real Chief reply](pair-the-pwa-and-get-a-real-chief-reply.md): The core journey — pair the app to the computer, say hello, Chief really answers (the `core` and `upgrade` journeys)
- [A pinned tool that failed](a-pinned-tool-that-failed.md): A helper's pinned kit tool fails; the step says the work did not happen and names the newer copy an update brings in
- [A hand-off that fails: Chief's lane is reset](handoff-lane-reset.md): A helper's own job fails; Chief's job settles at once in plain words and his lane is let go
- [Voice with Chief](voice.md): Tap the mic in Chief's box and speak, the words wait in the box; tap Talk, Chief's reply is read aloud with its line marked
- [Speaking with no on-device voice pack](phone-mic-fallback.md): Tap the mic on the Android app with no speech pack; the phone's speech service opens the mic
- [Chief on this phone with a MiniMax key](phone-only-minimax.md): Use this phone only, pick MiniMax, paste the key, pick the model; the chat names it
